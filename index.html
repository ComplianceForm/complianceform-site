What's inside

Twelve tabs, built around the checks UK premises are actually expected to carry out.

Dashboard

Every routine check with the date it was last done, when it is next due and a red, amber or green status.

Fire alarm log

Weekly call point tests, with drop-downs for the result and space for faults and actions.

Emergency lighting log

Monthly function tests and the annual full duration test, tracked separately.

Fire door register and checks

List every door once. Each inspection uses the five points from the Home Office fire door fact sheet and marks the door Pass or Fail.

Extinguisher register

Annual service dates per unit, plus a monthly visual check log.

Drills, training and defects

Drill times, staff refresher dates and a defects list that closes itself when you add the date fixed.

Built for the person who has to keep the records

Most fire safety log books are paper booklets or blank PDFs. They work until someone forgets to test the alarm or misses a fire door check, and nobody notices until an inspection.

This one is a spreadsheet that checks your records for you. Add each check as you do it and the dashboard shows what's coming up, what's overdue and which defects are still open.

  • Default intervals based on BS 5839-1, BS 5266-1, BS 5306-3 and the Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022, all of which you can change
  • Warns you a set number of days before anything falls due
  • Drop-down answers so records stay consistent between staff
  • Prints cleanly on A4 for when someone asks for a paper copy
  • No macros, no add-ons and no account to create
